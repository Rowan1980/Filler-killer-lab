Speech engine

On-device model v8 (170k parameters, 24 Oct 2026, trained with background noise, room echo and phone-mic effects, and listening to pitch steadiness as well as the sound) that listens for um/uh/er. No audio leaves your phone. On a 3.1-hour real podcast test (928 ums) it caught about 81% / 71% / 48% of ums at roughly 63 / 26 / 9 false alerts per hour (sensitive / balanced / strict). With noise added (café chatter, street, music, a room, a pocket) it still caught 57–66% at 25 false alerts per hour or fewer.
