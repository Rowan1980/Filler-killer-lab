Filler Killer support

Email king.rowan@gmail.com with a question, a problem or an idea. Say which iPhone you have and what happened.

It doesn’t buzz

Check Alerts on Practice has vibration on, and that the phone isn’t on silent if you use sound.

It misses ums, or buzzes too often

Change the sensitivity in Settings. Then, on Results, mark wrong buzzes Not an um, tap the waveform to add missed ones, and Save. After a few sessions, use Tune to fit it to your voice.

Words like “like” aren’t counted

Allow speech recognition in the iPhone Settings app › Filler Killer.
