Filler Killer privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026 · Filler Killer for iPhone, by Daydream Ventures

Short version: Filler Killer listens on your phone and keeps what you save on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on Deepgram, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking.

The microphone

While you practise, Filler Killer uses the microphone to catch filler words like “um”, “uh” and “like”. The sound is analysed on your iPhone by Filler Killer’s own detector and by Apple’s on-device speech recognition. It isn’t sent anywhere unless you turn on Deepgram (below).

What stays on your phone

  • The names of the people you add, your settings and your practice history (counts and dates).
  • Recordings of sessions you choose to Save, and the marks you make on them. Sessions you don’t save are discarded.
  • Your tuning, which is learned on the phone from your saved sessions.

We can’t see any of this. Deleting a session, a person or the app removes it.

Deepgram (optional)

If you choose Deepgram in the detector picker on Practice, or tap Check with Deepgram on a result, that audio is sent to Deepgram, a speech recognition service, to find filler words. It goes through our server, which passes it straight on and doesn’t store it. We ask Deepgram not to keep it or use it for training. Leave Deepgram off and no audio leaves your phone.

To stop overuse, our server remembers the network (IP) address of each request for up to an hour. It isn’t linked to you, saved, or shared.

The Filler Killer Band (optional)

If you pair a Filler Killer Band, the app uses Bluetooth to tell it when to tap your wrist. Only that signal is sent, directly from your phone to the band.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email or phone number.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no data sold or shared with anyone else.
  • No location, contacts or photos. (iOS asks every app to describe photo access; Filler Killer doesn’t use it.)

Children

Filler Killer doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes, for example if an option to share clips to improve the detector is added, it will be off unless you turn it on, the app will explain it first, and this page will be updated.
